# Why Netflix is betting on systems thinkers—not specialists—in the AI era | Elizabeth Stone (CPTO)

## Provider summary

Elizabeth Stone, Netflix's Product and Technology Officer, discusses the evolving landscape of roles in tech and entertainment due to AI advancements. She emphasizes the importance of maintaining craft excellence while adapting to new technologies, the need for systems thinking, and the ongoing necessity for specialized skills even as roles become more fluid.

- The emergence of AI has blurred traditional job roles, leading to confusion about responsibilities but also opportunities for faster prototyping and collaboration.
- Craft excellence in engineering, data science, and design remains crucial despite the rise of AI tools.
- Netflix's culture prioritizes high agency, autonomy, and a focus on excellence, which aligns with the operational strategies of leading AI labs.
- Systems thinking is increasingly important for navigating complex projects and ensuring cohesive product development across functions.
- The company is hiring more systems thinkers and emphasizing AI fluency across all roles, adapting career ladders to reflect these changes.
- AI is transforming data analysis and content production, enabling faster insights and creative ideation.
- The future of entertainment will involve diverse formats and personalized experiences, with AI playing a supportive role rather than replacing human creativity.
- Netflix aims to empower creators with flexible tools, fostering innovation while maintaining a human-centered approach to storytelling.
